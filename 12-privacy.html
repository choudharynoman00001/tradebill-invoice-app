Privacy

Your business details, clients, and documents are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They are not uploaded to our servers.

If you enter a Pro license key, the key is sent to Lemon Squeezy to check that it is valid. Payments and billing details are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy; we never see your card.

We do not sell data. We do not run advertising trackers in the app.
